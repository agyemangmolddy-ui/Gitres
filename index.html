TRUST IS THE FOUNDATION

Useful data should never come at the cost of privacy.

DataVault is designed around responsible sourcing. Providers must describe where data came from, confirm they have the right to share it and explain the licence. Personal or identifiable financial records are not permitted.

  • Provider source and licence declarations
  • Review before a listing is published
  • Private account credentials and protected routes
